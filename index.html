Our Safari Destinations

Discover Sri Lanka's Wild Heart

Choose between the world's highest leopard density park or Asia's grandest migratory bird sanctuary.

World Famous

Yala National Park (Block 1 & 5)

Renowned for having one of the highest leopard densities in the world. Yala offers dry woodland, grassy plains, and coastal lagoons teeming with wild Sri Lankan elephants, sloth bears, mugger crocodiles, and spotted deer.

Sri Lankan Leopard Sloth Bear Crocodiles
Birdwatcher's Haven

Bundala National Park

UNESCO Biosphere Reserve and Sri Lanka's premier birdwatching haven. Home to nearly 200 species of waterbirds, including thousands of winter migratory Greater Flamingos, pelicans, painted storks, and wild elephants.

Greater Flamingos Lagoons & Dunes 197 Bird Species
